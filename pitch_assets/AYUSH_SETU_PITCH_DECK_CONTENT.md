## Slide 4 - Feasibility and Viability

**Why this is feasible now**

- Built as a deployable full-stack prototype, not a mock-up: persisted users, roles, profiles, opportunities, applications, courses, certificates and analytics.
- Modular rollout: begin with 5-10 AYUSH institutions and verified industry partners; expand through a standard skill taxonomy and role templates.
- Uses familiar web technology and managed PostgreSQL, keeping hardware and onboarding overhead low.

**Risks and practical mitigations**

| Risk | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Inconsistent self-reported skills | Combine self-rating with MCQs, course outcomes, mentor evidence and verified badges. |
| Low-quality / stale postings | Partner verification, expiry dates, role templates, moderation and audit logs. |
| Skill taxonomy varies by system | Discipline-aware assessment banks for Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy; expert review panel. |
| Data privacy and access | JWT sessions, role-based endpoint protection, minimum-data approach and consent-led portfolio sharing. |
| Adoption friction | Start with career cell workflows and industry-created courses that give immediate learner value. |

**Rollout timeline graphic:**

- **0-3 months:** pilot, partner onboarding, validate skill taxonomy
- **3-6 months:** bridge courses, certificates, employer feedback loop
- **6-12 months:** multi-institution analytics and AIIA-level dashboards

**Speaker line (20 seconds):** “We deliberately made the platform modular. A college gets value from assessment and analytics on day one; industry gets ranked, evidence-backed candidates without waiting for a national-scale rollout.”

## Slide 5 - Impact and Benefits

**For learners**

- Makes career readiness visible beyond marks
- Gives a concrete upskilling path before applying
- Builds a shareable, verified portfolio of skills, courses and outcomes

**For institutions and faculty**

- Reveals batch-wise skill gaps against real opportunity requirements
- Converts placement and employer feedback into targeted curriculum actions
- Creates a unified channel for FDPs, research, mentorship and industry collaboration

**For industry and public systems**

- Shortens screening through explainable, skill-aligned matching
- Improves trust with verified course and credential evidence
- Provides aggregate demand-supply insight for evidence-led capacity building

**Success metrics for pilot:**

- Assessment completion and profile-verification rate
- % students receiving an actionable skill-gap recommendation
- Course completion and credential-verification rate
- Application-to-shortlist conversion by match band
- Employer response time and institution-level gap closure

**Impact statement:** “AYUSH Setu converts placement from a one-time event into a measurable, continuously improving education-to-employment system.”

**Speaker line (20 seconds):** “The impact is not merely more applications. It is better prepared learners, more confident employers, and institutions able to act on evidence rather than anecdote.”
